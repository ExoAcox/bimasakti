Spiral Galaxy

Milky Way Galaxy

The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy; home to our Solar System. It contains billions of stars, gas, and dust, with a supermassive black hole at its center.

Planetary System

Solar System

The Solar System is the gravitationally bound system of the Sun and the objects that orbit it, including eight planets, dwarf planets, and countless smaller bodies.

Alpha Centauri System

The closest star system to our Solar System, located about 4.37 light-years away. It consists of three stars: Alpha Centauri A, B, and Proxima Centauri.

TRAPPIST-1 System

An ultra-cool red dwarf star with seven Earth-sized terrestrial planets orbiting it. It hosts the largest number of habitable-zone Earth candidates.

Lich (PSR B1257+12)

A millisecond pulsar located 2,300 light-years away in Virgo. It is famous for hosting the first confirmed extrasolar planetary system discovered.
